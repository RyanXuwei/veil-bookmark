隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-15　適用版本：0.1.9 起

摘要

Veil Bookmark 是 Chrome 擴充功能，用來保存加密的私人書籤與記事。開發者沒有架設任何伺服器，不會收集、接收或販售你的任何資料。書籤在你的瀏覽器中以你的主密碼加密；若你選擇啟用 Google Drive 同步，上傳的也只有加密後的資料。

擴充功能處理的資料

  • 書籤與記事內容（名稱、網址、資料夾、標籤、筆記、記事標題與內文）：以 AES-256-GCM 加密後存在你電腦的 Chrome 擴充功能儲存空間。主密碼不會被儲存或傳送。
  • 目前分頁的標題與網址：只有在你按「收藏目前網頁」時才讀取，用來填入新書籤。
  • 設定（介面語言、同步狀態）：存在本機。
  • 解鎖狀態與 Google 存取權杖：只存在瀏覽器工作階段記憶體，上鎖或關閉瀏覽器即清除。

Google 使用者資料

啟用 Google Drive 同步時，擴充功能只請求一項權限：

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata：在你的 Google Drive「應用程式資料」隱藏資料夾中建立、讀取與刪除本擴充功能自己的檔案。

這項權限只用來保存與讀取你的加密書籤快照，以便在你的多台電腦之間同步。擴充功能無法看到你 Drive 中的其他檔案。資料直接在你的瀏覽器與 Google 之間傳輸，不經過開發者或任何第三方。

Veil Bookmark 對從 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與傳輸，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」（Limited Use）規定。我們不會將 Google 使用者資料用於廣告、不會轉讓或販售，也不會由任何人閱讀。

分享與第三方

不與任何人分享資料。擴充功能沒有分析、追蹤或廣告程式，也不載入外部網站圖示。

保存與刪除

  • 本機資料：在擴充功能內刪除書籤，或從 Chrome 移除擴充功能，即刪除本機加密資料。
  • Drive 資料：在擴充功能「同步與備份」按「中斷連線」停止同步；要刪除 Drive 上的加密快照，可到 Google Drive 網頁「設定 → 管理應用程式 → Veil Bookmark → 刪除隱藏的應用程式資料」。
  • 撤銷授權：到 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權移除 Veil Bookmark。

安全

加密在你的裝置上完成，金鑰由主密碼衍生（PBKDF2），開發者與 Google 都無法解密。遺失主密碼將無法復原資料，請妥善保存並定期匯出加密備份。

重要聲明（免責聲明）

  1. 主密碼只存在你的記憶中。Veil Bookmark 不會在任何地方（本機、Google Drive 或開發者）儲存、備份或傳送你的主密碼。
  2. 沒有任何密碼救援或重設機制，開發者也無法協助找回密碼或解密資料。
  3. 一旦遺失主密碼，所有加密的書籤與記事將永久無法開啟。
  4. 請自行妥善保管主密碼，並定期匯出加密備份；備份檔同樣需要主密碼才能還原。
  5. 本軟體依「現狀」提供，不提供任何明示或默示擔保。在法律允許的範圍內，開發者對資料遺失、無法存取或其他損害不負任何責任。

兒童

本擴充功能不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會刻意收集兒童資料。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-15 · Applies to version 0.1.9 and later

Summary

Veil Bookmark is a Chrome extension for keeping encrypted private bookmarks and notes. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, receive or sell any of your data. Bookmarks are encrypted in your browser with your master password; if you turn on Google Drive sync, only encrypted data is uploaded.

Data the extension handles

  • Bookmark and note content (title, URL, folder, tags, notes, note titles and text): encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. Your master password is never stored or transmitted.
  • Current tab title and URL: read only when you click "Save current page", to fill in the new bookmark.
  • Settings (interface language, sync status): stored locally.
  • Unlock state and Google access token: kept only in browser session memory and cleared when you lock or close the browser.

Google user data

When Google Drive sync is enabled, the extension requests a single permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: create, read and delete the extension's own files in the hidden application data folder of your Google Drive.

This permission is used only to store and read your encrypted bookmark snapshots so they stay in sync across your computers. The extension cannot see any other files in your Drive. Data travels directly between your browser and Google, never through the developer or any third party.

Veil Bookmark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not transferred or sold, and is not read by any human.

Sharing and third parties

No data is shared with anyone. The extension contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code and does not load external site icons.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data: deleting bookmarks in the extension, or removing the extension from Chrome, deletes the local encrypted data.
  • Drive data: click "Disconnect" under Sync & backup to stop syncing. To delete the encrypted snapshots in Drive, open Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Veil Bookmark → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke access: remove Veil Bookmark under Third-party access in your Google Account.

Security

Encryption happens on your device with a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2); neither the developer nor Google can decrypt it. A lost master password cannot be recovered, so keep it safe and export encrypted backups regularly.

Important notice (disclaimer)

  1. Your master password exists only in your memory. Veil Bookmark never stores, backs up or transmits it anywhere — not on this device, in Google Drive, or with the developer.
  2. There is no password recovery or reset, and the developer cannot help recover your password or decrypt your data.
  3. If you lose your master password, all encrypted bookmarks and notes become permanently inaccessible.
  4. Keep your master password safe and export encrypted backups regularly; backups also need the master password to restore.
  5. This software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied. To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for any data loss, loss of access or other damages.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data.
