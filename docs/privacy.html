隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-17（最後更新：手機版搬到獨立網址）　適用版本：0.2.0 起

摘要

Veil Bookmark 是 Chrome 擴充功能，用來保存加密的私人書籤與記事。開發者沒有架設任何伺服器，不會收集、接收或販售你的任何資料。書籤在你的瀏覽器中以你的主密碼加密；若你選擇啟用 Google Drive 同步，上傳的也只有加密後的資料。

擴充功能處理的資料

  • 書籤與記事內容（名稱、網址、資料夾、標籤、私密備註、記事標題與內文）：以 AES-256-GCM 加密後存在你電腦的 Chrome 擴充功能儲存空間。主密碼不會被儲存或傳送。
  • 救援金鑰：建立時只在畫面上顯示一次，不會被儲存或傳送；保險庫中只保存以救援金鑰加密的資料金鑰。
  • 記事中的圖片（1.0.0 起）：你貼上、拖曳或選擇的圖片會在你的電腦上縮小並重新編碼，不保留拍攝地點等中繼資料，與記事一起加密保存。貼上網頁內容時，擴充功能不會從網路下載其中的外部圖片。
  • 列印／另存 PDF：使用 Chrome 的列印視窗在你的電腦上產生，擴充功能不會傳送文件內容。產生的 PDF 不加密，請自行妥善保管。
  • 目前分頁的標題與網址：只有在你按「收藏目前網頁」時才讀取，用來填入新書籤。
  • 設定（介面語言、同步狀態）：存在本機。
  • 解鎖狀態與 Google 存取權杖：只存在瀏覽器工作階段記憶體，上鎖或關閉瀏覽器即清除。

Google 使用者資料

0.2.4 起，記事也可包含紙張配色、手繪筆畫與錄音播放貼紙的位置。只有按「開始錄音」並允許麥克風後才會錄音；停止或上鎖會關閉麥克風。完成並放置的錄音隨記事加密保存在本機，啟用 Drive 同步時才隨加密保險庫上傳。不提供語音辨識，也不把原始聲音傳給開發者。可個別刪除錄音。

啟用 Google Drive 同步時，擴充功能只請求一項權限：

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata：在你的 Google Drive「應用程式資料」隱藏資料夾中建立、讀取與刪除本擴充功能自己的檔案。

這項權限只用來保存與讀取你的加密書籤快照，以便在你的多台電腦之間同步。擴充功能無法看到你 Drive 中的其他檔案。資料直接在你的瀏覽器與 Google 之間傳輸，不經過開發者或任何第三方。

1.2.0 起，在新電腦首次開啟時，如果這個 Chrome 登入的 Google 帳號先前已授權 Veil Bookmark，擴充功能會列出上述隱藏資料夾，確認是否有可還原的加密備份（只檢查是否存在，不下載內容）。若尚未授權，不會跳出 Google 授權視窗，只有你按「檢查 Google Drive」時才會詢問。

Veil Bookmark 對從 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與傳輸，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」（Limited Use）規定。我們不會將 Google 使用者資料用於廣告、不會轉讓或販售，也不會由任何人閱讀。

手機版網頁

1.7.0 起可保存多個 Google 帳戶。透過相同 drive.appdata 權限的 Drive about API 取得帳戶識別碼、電子郵件與顯示名稱，用於顯示及核對工作區。帳戶資料與短期存取權杖保存在本機瀏覽器，加密副本與生物辨識設定依帳戶分開保存；分頁各自選擇帳戶。移除帳戶只清除該帳戶的本機資料，不刪除 Drive 資料，也不登出其他 Google 服務。

手機版（https://veil-bookmark.bitingbox.win/）是由 Cloudflare 託管的靜態網頁（獨立網址，不與其他網頁共用），供無法安裝擴充功能的手機使用。它以同一個 drive.appdata 權限讀寫同一份加密快照，主密碼只在手機的瀏覽器中使用，解密後的資料不會傳到任何伺服器。

  • Google 存取權杖（約 1 小時有效，只能存取 Drive 的隱藏應用程式資料夾）保存在這支手機的瀏覽器中直到過期，重新整理或重新開啟時不必再次連線；過期後會向 Google 重新取得，不需要再按一次。在「同步與設定」清除資料並登出時會一併刪除。
  • 手機會在瀏覽器的 IndexedDB 保留一份加密副本，供離線開啟；可在「同步與設定」清除，不影響 Drive 與電腦上的資料。
  • 選用的生物辨識解鎖（Face ID／指紋）：手機建立一把「通行金鑰」，由手機的生物辨識保護；它產生的密鑰把保險庫的資料金鑰加密後存在這支手機。主密碼與生物特徵都不會被儲存或傳送；可隨時在設定中停用。
  • 在手機上撰寫記事時，只有你選擇插入的照片會被讀取；照片在手機上重新編碼（只保留像素，移除拍攝位置等中繼資料）後與記事一起加密。
  • 不載入任何外部程式碼、字型、分析或廣告；開啟書籤時不傳送來源網址（no-referrer）。
  • 網頁由 Cloudflare 託管，Cloudflare 可能依其政策記錄連線資訊（例如 IP 位址），但看不到你的書籤內容。

分享與第三方

不與任何人分享資料。擴充功能沒有分析、追蹤或廣告程式，也不載入外部網站圖示。

保存與刪除

  • 本機資料：在擴充功能內刪除書籤，或從 Chrome 移除擴充功能，即刪除本機加密資料。
  • Drive 資料：在擴充功能「同步與備份」按「中斷連線」停止同步；要刪除 Drive 上的加密快照，可到 Google Drive 網頁「設定 → 管理應用程式 → Veil Bookmark → 刪除隱藏的應用程式資料」。
  • 撤銷授權：到 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權移除 Veil Bookmark。

安全

加密在你的裝置上完成。資料以隨機資料金鑰加密，資料金鑰再由主密碼（PBKDF2）與救援金鑰各加密一份，開發者與 Google 都無法解密。

重要聲明（免責聲明）

  1. 主密碼與救援金鑰只由你保管。Veil Bookmark 不會在任何地方（本機、Google Drive 或開發者）儲存或傳送你的主密碼或救援金鑰。
  2. 忘記主密碼時，唯一的救援方式是使用救援金鑰；開發者無法協助找回密碼、救援金鑰或解密資料。
  3. 主密碼與救援金鑰都遺失時，所有加密的書籤與記事將永久無法開啟。
  4. 救援金鑰與主密碼同樣重要：取得救援金鑰的人可以重設密碼並開啟你的資料。請離線妥善保管，並定期匯出加密備份。
  5. 本軟體依「現狀」提供，不提供任何明示或默示擔保。在法律允許的範圍內，開發者對資料遺失、無法存取或其他損害不負任何責任。

兒童

本擴充功能不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會刻意收集兒童資料。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-17 (last updated) · Applies to version 0.2.0 and later

Summary

Veil Bookmark is a Chrome extension for keeping encrypted private bookmarks and notes. The developer runs no servers and does not collect, receive or sell any of your data. Bookmarks are encrypted in your browser with your master password; if you turn on Google Drive sync, only encrypted data is uploaded.

Data the extension handles

From version 0.2.4, notes can include paper colors, drawings and positioned audio stickers. Recording starts only when you select Record and grant microphone access. Stopping or locking closes the microphone. Completed and placed clips are encrypted with the note locally, and included in encrypted vault sync only if you enable Drive. There is no speech recognition or transmission of raw audio to the developer. You can delete clips individually.

  • Bookmark and note content (title, URL, folder, tags, notes, note titles and text): encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. Your master password is never stored or transmitted.
  • Recovery key: shown on screen once when created and never stored or transmitted; the vault only keeps the data key encrypted with it.
  • Pictures in notes (from 1.0.0): pictures you paste, drop or choose are resized and re-encoded on your computer, without metadata such as location, and encrypted with the note. When you paste web content, the extension does not download external pictures from the internet.
  • Print / Save as PDF: produced on your computer by Chrome's print dialog; the extension does not send the document anywhere. The resulting PDF is not encrypted, so keep it safe.
  • Current tab title and URL: read only when you click "Save current page", to fill in the new bookmark.
  • Settings (interface language, sync status): stored locally.
  • Unlock state and Google access token: kept only in browser session memory and cleared when you lock or close the browser.

Google user data

When Google Drive sync is enabled, the extension requests a single permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: create, read and delete the extension's own files in the hidden application data folder of your Google Drive.

This permission is used only to store and read your encrypted bookmark snapshots so they stay in sync across your computers. The extension cannot see any other files in your Drive. Data travels directly between your browser and Google, never through the developer or any third party.

From version 1.2.0, when the extension is first opened on a new computer and the Google account signed in to Chrome has already authorised Veil Bookmark, it lists that hidden folder to see whether there is an encrypted backup to restore (it only checks that one exists and downloads nothing). If you have not authorised it, no Google prompt is shown; you are asked only when you click "Check Google Drive".

Veil Bookmark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not transferred or sold, and is not read by any human.

Mobile web version

From 1.7.0, multiple Google accounts can be kept locally. Drive about, using the same drive.appdata scope, supplies the account identifier, email and display name to label and verify workspaces. Account details and short-lived access tokens stay in the browser; encrypted copies and biometric settings are isolated per account. Tabs select accounts independently. Removing an account deletes only its local data, not Drive data or Google service sessions.

The mobile version (https://veil-bookmark.bitingbox.win/) is a static page hosted by Cloudflare at its own address (shared with no other pages) for phones that cannot install the extension. It reads and writes the same encrypted snapshots with the same drive.appdata permission. Your master password is used only in your phone's browser, and decrypted data is never sent to any server.

  • The Google access token (valid for about one hour, limited to Drive's hidden app folder) is kept in this phone's browser until it expires, so reloading or reopening does not ask you to connect again; after that a new one is requested from Google without another tap. Removing data and signing out under Sync & settings deletes it.
  • The phone keeps an encrypted copy in the browser's IndexedDB so it opens offline. You can remove it under Sync & settings; Drive and your computer are not affected.
  • Optional biometric unlock (Face ID / fingerprint): the phone creates a passkey protected by its biometrics; a secret it produces encrypts the vault's data key, which stays on this phone. Neither your master password nor your biometric data is stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off in settings at any time.
  • When you write notes on the phone, only the photos you choose to insert are read; they are re-encoded on the phone (pixels only, without metadata such as location) and encrypted with the note.
  • No external code, fonts, analytics or ads are loaded; opening a bookmark sends no referrer.
  • The page is hosted by Cloudflare, which may log connection details such as IP addresses under its own policy, but cannot see your bookmarks.

Sharing and third parties

No data is shared with anyone. The extension contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code and does not load external site icons.

Retention and deletion

  • Local data: deleting bookmarks in the extension, or removing the extension from Chrome, deletes the local encrypted data.
  • Drive data: click "Disconnect" under Sync & backup to stop syncing. To delete the encrypted snapshots in Drive, open Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Veil Bookmark → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke access: remove Veil Bookmark under Third-party access in your Google Account.

Security

Encryption happens on your device. Data is encrypted with a random data key, which is in turn encrypted by your master password (PBKDF2) and by your recovery key; neither the developer nor Google can decrypt it.

Important notice (disclaimer)

  1. Only you hold your master password and recovery key. Veil Bookmark never stores or transmits either of them anywhere — not on this device, in Google Drive, or with the developer.
  2. If you forget your master password, the recovery key is the only way back. The developer cannot recover your password or recovery key, or decrypt your data.
  3. If you lose both your master password and your recovery key, all encrypted bookmarks and notes become permanently inaccessible.
  4. Your recovery key is as important as your master password: anyone who has it can reset your password and open your data. Keep it offline and safe, and export encrypted backups regularly.
  5. This software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied. To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for any data loss, loss of access or other damages.
